What Caster provides

Caster sends a video you choose to a supported screen on your Wi-Fi. On iPhone and iPad, Caster casts to AirPlay, Chromecast, and Roku. Roku uses manual IP setup. Caster does not support DLNA on iOS, Miracast, multi-cast, or Android AirPlay. iPhone and iPad send; they do not act as a Caster receiver.

You may pick a local video with the system picker or provide a video link. Video Assist is an in-app browser. It casts only when the page exposes a direct supported video. Protected streams, blob sources, and ordinary web pages cannot be cast. Caster does not download videos or extract subtitles.

Screen mirroring requires another device running Caster with Receiver Mode on Android. Mirroring is not AirPlay, Chromecast, Roku, or DLNA screen sharing.

Wi-Fi is required. Compatibility depends on the receiver and the media. Caster does not promise every television, every web video, or a latency number.
