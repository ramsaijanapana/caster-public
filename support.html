Support

For help with Caster, email devaraventures@gmail.com or open a support issue. Please include your device model, OS version, whether you used AirPlay, Chromecast, Roku, or mirroring, and a description of what happened. Do not attach private videos or purchase receipts in a public issue.

Using Caster

  • Local video: pick a video, then choose AirPlay, Chromecast, or a Roku added by IP. Remain in the foreground while Caster is serving a picked file.
  • Video Assist: open a page that exposes a direct supported video. Protected, blob, or page-only sources should show an unavailable reason, not a sample clip.
  • Mirroring: requires a second device running Caster with Receiver Mode on Android. iOS is a sender only.
  • Roku on iOS uses manual IP setup. iOS does not support DLNA, Miracast, or multi-cast.

Purchases

If Premium access is missing after a purchase, use the store restore-purchases control in Caster. Subscriptions can be managed in your Apple Account or Google Play subscription settings. Apple and Google handle billing and refund requests.
