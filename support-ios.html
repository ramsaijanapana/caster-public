Caster Support for iPhone and iPad

Ask a question or request help

Devara Ventures provides support for Caster: Video Casting. Email us at devaraventures@gmail.com. You can copy this address into any email service. You do not need a Caster account to request help.

Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, Caster version, the steps you tried, and any error message. For casting problems, include your receiver model and whether you used AirPlay, Chromecast, or Roku. Please avoid sending private videos or passwords.

You may also open a support issue on GitHub if you have a GitHub account. Issues are public; use email for private support requests.

Playing a video link

Open Link expects a direct video file or supported stream, such as an HTTPS MP4 or HLS link. A link to a webpage containing a video is different from the video file itself. Use Video Assist for webpages that expose a supported direct video.

If a video does not play, check that the link still exists and is accessible without signing in. Expired links, protected streams, and blob sources cannot be played directly. You can also pick a video from your library.

Casting a local video

Connect your phone and receiver to the same Wi-Fi. Pick a video, then choose AirPlay, Chromecast, or a Roku added by IP. Keep Caster open while it serves a selected file to the receiver. Roku setup requires its local IP address.

Screen mirroring

Mirroring requires a separate compatible device running Caster in Receiver Mode on the same Wi-Fi. iPhone and iPad send the screen; they do not act as Caster receivers. AirPlay, Chromecast, and Roku do not receive the Caster mirroring stream.
