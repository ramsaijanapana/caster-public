Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

Caster is developed and operated by Devara Ventures. This policy explains how the Caster iOS and Android apps handle information. Caster does not require a Caster login or account.

Local media and screen mirroring

When you pick a local video, Caster serves that file over your Wi-Fi to the device you choose using a short-lived local HTTP relay. Caster does not upload that file to a Caster server. A television cannot read a phone-local file path. Screen mirroring captures your display only while you are actively mirroring and sends encoded frames to a Caster receiver you choose on the same Wi-Fi using SRT without application-layer encryption. Caster does not store your screen, media, or device list on Caster servers.

Caster does not claim that nothing ever leaves the device. Not all data transmitted off the device is encrypted in transit. The local HTTP video relay and SRT mirror path are not HTTPS. Google Cast may process Chromecast diagnostics as described below; that is separate from the local media path. Caster does not claim that every Cast-related packet on your LAN is HTTPS.

Local network and Bluetooth

Caster uses your local network to discover TVs and streaming devices and to serve the video you select. On iOS, Caster may use Bluetooth because the Google Cast SDK is linked, to help discover nearby Cast devices.

Google Cast (Chromecast)

When you cast to a Chromecast, Google’s Cast SDK may process data so Google can operate Chromecast discovery and sessions. On iOS, the Cast SDK 4.8.6 privacy manifest declares device identifiers, product interaction, and other diagnostic data for analytics and app functionality, not linked to your identity and not used for tracking. On Android, this app’s Play Data safety form declares Cast-related telemetry as app interactions used for analytics (required; not shared; not ephemeral) and does not select Play Device IDs or crash logs. Videos you pick or mirror are declared separately as optional app functionality. Caster does not operate a developer backend that receives or stores this Cast SDK telemetry. The integrated Google Cast SDK in the app does process it, which is why store forms treat it as data collected from the app. Google processes Cast information under Google’s policies. Store nutrition labels differ by platform and are not a single shared taxonomy.

Purchases and accounts

This free launch has no Caster login, no in-app purchase, and no paywall. Features are not gated on StoreKit or Google Play Billing. Caster does not collect purchase history. An optional purchase-verifier exists in source and stays inactive in this launch because no Caster backend URL is configured.

App preferences

Caster stores on-device preferences such as onboarding and settings using the app’s own storage (UserDefaults on iOS). That information stays in the app container unless you back up or reset the device.

What Caster does not do

Caster has no Caster-operated user account, advertising SDK, or tracking domains in the shipped privacy manifest. Caster does not sell personal information. Video Assist is an in-app browser that can open public HTTPS pages you choose; those pages are not uploaded to Caster.

Platform services

Apple and Google may process store listing, device, diagnostic, backup, file-picker, or network information under their own policies. Those platform services are separate from Caster’s developer-operated systems. Caster currently does not operate a developer backend in default Release builds.

Removing data

Uninstalling Caster removes its local app container according to the operating system. Local videos you picked remain in your photo or files library. This free launch does not create Caster store purchases to retain.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if Caster’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to devaraventures@gmail.com or by opening a support issue. Do not include personal videos, device identifiers, or purchase receipts in a public issue.
