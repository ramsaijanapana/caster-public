Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

Caster is developed and operated by Devara Ventures. This policy explains how the Caster iOS and Android apps handle information. Caster does not require a Caster account.

Local media and screen mirroring

When you pick a local video, Caster serves that file over your Wi-Fi to the device you choose. Caster does not upload that file to a Caster server. A television cannot read a phone-local file path, so a short-lived local relay is used. Screen mirroring captures your display only while you are actively mirroring and sends encoded frames to a Caster receiver you choose on the same Wi-Fi. Caster does not store your screen, media, or device list on Caster servers.

Caster does not claim that nothing ever leaves the device. Google Cast may process Chromecast diagnostics as described below.

Local network and Bluetooth

Caster uses your local network to discover TVs and streaming devices and to serve the video you select. On iOS, Caster may use Bluetooth because the Google Cast SDK is linked, to help discover nearby Cast devices.

Google Cast (Chromecast)

When you cast to a Chromecast, the Google Cast SDK may process device identifiers, product interaction, and other diagnostic data for analytics and app functionality so Google can operate Chromecast discovery and sessions. Caster does not collect or store those Cast SDK fields. Those fields are not used by Caster for tracking. Google processes Cast information under Google’s policies.

Purchases

Optional Caster Premium is processed by Apple StoreKit on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android. Purchase tokens and entitlement state are processed locally to gate premium features. Caster does not store purchase data on Caster servers. Apple and Google process store-account purchase information under their own policies. An optional HTTPS purchase-verifier exists in source and stays inactive unless a Caster backend URL is configured; default Release builds do not send purchase data to a Caster server.

App preferences

Caster stores on-device preferences such as onboarding and settings using the app’s own storage (UserDefaults on iOS). That information stays in the app container unless you back up or reset the device.

What Caster does not do

Caster has no Caster-operated user account, advertising SDK, or tracking domains in the shipped privacy manifest. Caster does not sell personal information. Video Assist is an in-app browser that can open public HTTPS pages you choose; those pages are not uploaded to Caster.

Platform services

Apple and Google may process store, purchase, device, diagnostic, backup, file-picker, or network information under their own policies. Those platform services are separate from Caster’s developer-operated systems. Caster currently does not operate a developer backend in default Release builds.

Removing data

Uninstalling Caster removes its local app container according to the operating system. Local videos you picked remain in your photo or files library. Store purchases remain in your Apple or Google account until you manage them there.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if Caster’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to devaraventures@gmail.com or by opening a support issue. Do not include personal videos, device identifiers, or purchase receipts in a public issue.
